How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity. Growth clamps the seven-day TVL change to −50…+150% and counts 60% of it, so a protocol growing 20% reaches 112% of its base and one shrinking 20% keeps 88%. Maturity runs 0.70 for a new listing to 1.20 at four years, because a protocol that has held value for years with no token has proved it can hold value without one. Age unknown means 365 days. No name is ever special-cased: the same arithmetic scores every row, and each detail view recomputes it from the three inputs.

Scope. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL that record no token and are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades. The board adds every other rankable pre-token protocol the snapshot holds — 20 above the floor the watchlist leaves out, and 138 below it — so the radar finds future launches early.

Reading the record. “Records no token” means the snapshot's DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for the protocol. It is the record, not proof: a protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. The Live tokens tab shows, separately, the protocols whose token already trades, at the market's own value — never mixed into the ranking.

Farm plans. Each plan is generated from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age: the capital, the hold time and the weekly cadence follow those figures, and every step states what it costs and how long it takes. Nothing here is financial advice, and a launch is never promised.
